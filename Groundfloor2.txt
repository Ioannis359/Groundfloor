Realities -> 2d spaces
Cell -> Acting entity inside Realities

-> Simulate a simplified version of life and see how Cells react to new Realities(The 13th floor)
-> Cells are a feedback loop:
--> Its state get fed into a neural net
--> A neural net/markov chain/state mashine produces an action
--> That action get executed and changes its state
--> That neural net is basically its personality
--> To be able to observe its enviroment a half circle on the front will act as eyes.

-> Realities should be a simplified version of cities
--> There are Jobs, Resting Places, Eating Places and Entertainment Places
--> All those things are in seperate places distanced from each other
--> At the edge of all Realities the Simulation is visible but everyone avoids it (becasue i will progremm them that way)

-> Cell State(all Values are stored as a float | they cannot be smaller then 0 | the have no upper bound | 
                When a Value gets increased the final Value will be TanH(Value + Increase) * (1/Tanh(1))):
--> Ignorance (Opposite of Knowledge| get decreased by seeing the edge)
--> Saneness (gets increased by time, Rest and entertainment a little | gets decreased by Ignorance decrease)
--> Rest (Sleep | gets increased by Resting places| gets decreased by time and job)
--> Money (To buy food or entertainment, Gets increased by job)
--> Satiety (Opposite of Hunger | gets increased by food | gets decreased by time)
--> Vitality (Opposite of Burnout | gets decreased by little Rest | gets increased by sleep and entertainment)

-> Actions:
--> Suicide
--> Kill (Kills a Cell with an saneness level closest to 1 - this cells saneness level) <- The Idea is one often kills becasue the victim is either insane or one itself is insane
--> Work
--> Sleep
--> Entertain
--> Eat
--> Teach (Teaches cells (in vision) knowledge about the sim | decreases your Saneness by an (1/8) of your knowledge | increases observing cells knowledge by ((1/100) | increases with bad state | decreases when distanced ) of your knowledge)
--> Distract (Prevents all cells in Vision from execuitng their current and following Action | additionally has the effects of Teaching)
--> Distance (from a person)
