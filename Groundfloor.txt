Groundfloor: cellular automata * The 13th floor

Rules:
-> Realities are 2d spaces nodes can exist in.
-> Nodes have a insanity, knowledge, position, memory, common task Value
--> Memory:
---> Nodes can take actions. For example they can kill another node.
---> The all actions done by a node are recorded and affect chnaces of futures actions and their insanity and knowledge value.
--> Common task:
---> Basically a job.
---> Affects the holders knowledge if its a computer sience job that has acces to other simulations(Enum).
--> Position:
---> Realities are not infinite. The more one is closer to the edge of its Reality the more knowledge it gets(2 Vars, ranging from -1.0 to 1.0)
--> Knowledge:
---> How mush one knows about its simulated Reality(Ranges from 0.0 to 1.0).
---> Can only increase.
---> The higher the knowledge is the higher is the minimum insanity.
--> Insanity:
---> The faster knowledge rises the more insane one becomes(Ranges from 0.0 to 1.0).
---> Decreases very slowly.

-> Nodes take actions based on those Values.
--> Actions:
---> Killing:
----> Only in high insanity score.
----> The more often someone killed the more lower the minimum required insanity is to kill
----> Kills nodes with high knowledge
---> working:
----> If the job is comp. researcher then knowledge increases, else nothing happens
---> psycho. break down:
----> Happens when knowledge and insanity increase to rapidly.
---> switching realities:
----> can only be done by comp researchers.
---> ...
